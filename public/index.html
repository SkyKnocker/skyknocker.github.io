About Me

From taking apart toys as a kid to repairing fiber optic lines in the Army, I have always been obsessed with understanding the mechanics of the world around me. As a veteran of both the Marine Corps and the Army, I’ve spent years working with the hardware of the world: electronics, heavy machinery, and micro-circuitry.
